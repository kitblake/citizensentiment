# Citizen Sentiment

<img width="720" height="450" alt="SplashScreen" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8271da1d-bbe7-4345-8b33-eca3425b3c2f" />

Citizen Sentiment is a simple app that enables Kesennuma city officials to gather the preferences of citizens about a future project. It’s primarily visual where images, in A/B format, are distributed in multiple media, including web, app, and paper. Promotion includes a QR code link. After citizens scan the QR code they land on a poll page in the Citizen Sentiment app. They make a choice between image A and B and submit their preference. The city can then gauge the sentiment of the populace when making design decisions.
